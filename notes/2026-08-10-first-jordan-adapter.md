# First Jordan source adapter

I chose Jordanian unemployment as the first real adapter because the Department of Statistics publishes a dated release PDF and because unemployment vintages matter for later forecasting work.

The Q1 2026 document could be captured and parsed. The harder problem appeared after parsing: I do not yet have a settled basis for redistributing the full historical transcription, and the independent 20-cell comparison has not been signed.

Decision: keep the capture receipt, parser, source link, and blind review packet public; keep the official row quarantined; do not let a passing parser or test suite stand in for permission and independent checking.

Next question: whether the publisher can clarify reuse and whether the historical chart can be reconstructed from separately released tables without redistributing restricted source material.
