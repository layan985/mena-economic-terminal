# Roadmap: from alpha to regional operating system

## v0.1 — contract and reference engine (current)

- Canonical observation schema.
- Vintage-safe query semantics.
- Python/CLI/API reference surface.
- Synthetic fixtures and release blocker.
- Source registry and governance.

## v0.2 — Jordan macro/monetary pilot

- Onboard Central Bank of Jordan and Department of Statistics releases after terms review.
- Capture release calendars and timezone evidence.
- Publish inflation, labor, policy-rate, FX and selected monetary series.
- Independent 20-row-per-series document audit.
- Publish release diffs and a prospective vintage ledger.

Exit criterion: at least five verified series, two independently audited release cycles and no fixture rows in the public snapshot.

## v0.3 — cross-country core

- Add Egypt, Morocco, Tunisia, Saudi Arabia and UAE source stewards.
- Harmonized labor and inflation views without erasing national definitions.
- ILOSTAT, IMF and World Bank as labeled harmonized layers.
- UN Comtrade pipeline retaining classification and estimation metadata.

## v0.4 — markets and firms

- Central-bank decision event ledger and policy documents.
- Yield curves, sovereign spreads, FX and commodities under rights-compatible access.
- Corporate filings, entity aliases and effective-dated ownership graph.
- Firm AI-adoption evidence with blinded labeling and adjudication.

## v0.5 — labor and text infrastructure

- Licensed job-posting collection with deduplication and occupation taxonomy.
- Versioned Arabic/English policy-document corpus.
- Geopolitical event metadata with source disagreement and uncertainty.

## v1.0 — public operating system

- Stable `pip install menaecon` release and versioned REST/Arrow API.
- Public status page, service-level targets and correction ledger.
- At least three external institutional users, two external maintainers and one independent full reproduction.
- Prospective vintage history long enough for genuine real-time macro evaluation.

## Priority rule

Source depth beats feature breadth. A series is not “done” when it appears in a chart; it is done when its release process, revisions, provenance, rights and independent validation are reproducible.
