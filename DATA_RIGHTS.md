# Data rights and third-party material

The repository-level MIT license covers software and documentation authored by the MENA Open Data & Evidence Lab unless a file states otherwise. It does not grant rights in third-party publications, source documents or statistical material.

Jordan Department of Statistics material is not covered by the MIT software license. Its website terms state that site information is subject to copyright unless noted otherwise, and no open redistribution grant was identified for the Q1 2026 unemployment release.

Accordingly:

- the original Jordan DoS PDF is not distributed here;
- the 51-cell chart transcription is retained outside the public tree while reuse permission is unresolved;
- the public repository retains only the canonical source link, capture metadata, SHA-256 receipt, parser code and an unsigned blind review packet;
- no Jordan observation may be labeled `verified` or included in a public data release until rights and independent-review gates pass.

If Jordan DoS grants written permission, the permission date, scope, attribution requirements and applicable license will be recorded before the affected data are restored. If permission is denied or remains unclear, the Terminal will continue to link to the official source without redistributing the source artifact or rights-pending transcription.
